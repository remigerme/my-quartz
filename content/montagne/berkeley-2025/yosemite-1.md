Two kinda crazy things here:

- Yosemite valley itself of course
- how I met the group I joined

Remember my outing from the weekend before at [[mt-diablo|Mount Diablo]]? Well, turns out Nathan and Will were about to do The Nose, and Sarah was looking for someone to go there with her. I was looking for people to go to Yosemite with... perfect fit!

The original plan was to hike up to the top of El Cap to cheer Nathan and Will, and then rappel down with them, but they summited one day earlier than expected. Instead, we rented some bikes and toured the valley.

As Will and I finished way before the others (Munginella was unsurprisingly crowded), we went to El Cap Meadows and talked about the Nose (waiting for some climbers to do the King Swing - rangers had set up a spyglass... we had to leave before though). So now, I should know about Dolt Tower, El Cap Tower, Texas Flake, Boot Flake, King Swing, Camp 4, The Great Roof, ...
